Primordial Paradigm is an independent game studio.

Its first game, [Ninefold Chaos](/games/ninefold-chaos/), is an action-adventure at the concept stage. The [development journal](/games/ninefold-chaos/journal/) follows it as it takes shape.

The studio also builds dashboards and data systems for clients, taken on selectively. See [work for hire](/services/).

You can follow the studio on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@irongod6947) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/primordialparadigm), or write to [contact@primordialparadigm.com](mailto:contact@primordialparadigm.com).
