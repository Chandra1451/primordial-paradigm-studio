Ninefold Chaos is the studio's first game. Right now it is a concept: an action-adventure with a name, and a lot still undecided.

The biggest open question is how it plays. More than one style of play is being considered, and none is settled yet. Rather than describe a game that does not exist, this journal will record decisions as they are made, along with what was tried and what was set aside.

Entries will be short and written when there is something real to say. There is no schedule.
